Student Ambassador Toolkit & Project Philosophy ⭐

Designed for Student Wellbeing Ambassadors (SWAs), campus peer mentors, and inclusive support teams to introduce incoming offer-holders to STARPath.

Why We Built STARPath

The transition from school or college to university is one of the most critical inflection points in higher education. For students with disabilities, ADHD, autism, dyslexia, or mental health conditions, this transition is often clouded by deep anxiety about accommodation privacy, sensory noise, exam adjustments, and campus navigation.

Four Engineering Commitments:

🔒 Zero Personal Data Logged

Names, UK phone numbers, and student IDs are wiped via deterministic regex before any search.

🛡️ Deterministic Crisis Guardrail

Expressions of distress bypass search entirely and immediately signpost 5 UK & UCLan emergency services.

⚖️ Zero Fabrication / Non-Hallucination

If information is not present in approved UCLan documents, STARPath gives an honest refusal.

👁️ WCAG 2.2 AA Accessibility

14:1+ High contrast mode, OpenDyslexic typography, Web Speech read-aloud, and keyboard navigation.

⚠️ Academic Prototype Notice

As specified in the university assignment brief for CO2007, this software is an academic proof-of-concept. It has been empirically benchmarked and tested with enrolled students and academic tutors. It operates alongside—never replacing—compassionate human student ambassadors.
