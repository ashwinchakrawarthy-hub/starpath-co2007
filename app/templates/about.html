About the STARPath Assistant

STARPath is a specialized proof-of-concept question-and-answer assistant built by an Agile student development team for CO2007 The Agile Professional at the University of Central Lancashire.

What STARPath Does:

  • Answers Common Questions: Provides direct answers to recurring questions about the 3-day summer residential, accessible halls, and disability support.
  • Shows Source Citations: Every answer clearly shows the official UCLan web page and section it came from so students can verify details.
  • Plain-English Glossary: Explains confusing UK university terms (like ISP, DSA, or En-suite) in simple words for international students.
  • Guaranteed Honesty: If an answer is not in the official pages, it says so directly rather than guessing or making things up.
  • Crisis Safety: Automatically routes any student expressing distress or self-harm to the UCLan Wellbeing Service and emergency human support.

⚠️ Academic Prototype Notice

As specified in the university assignment brief, this software is a student research prototype. It has been tested exclusively with enrolled module students and academic tutors. It is not deployed to real incoming offer-holders on the live STAR residential without formal institutional ethics approval.
