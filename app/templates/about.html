Why We Created STARPath ⭐

A student-led project built by second-year undergraduates to help incoming disabled, neurodivergent, and anxious offer-holders feel at home at UCLan before day one.

The Real Problem We Set Out to Solve

Moving from school or college to university is exciting, but it can also be terrifying. When we arrived at UCLan as first-years, we remember staring at 30-page induction handbooks, wondering:

  • "Will I have my own bathroom, or do I have to share with six strangers?"
  • "What if I get overwhelmed by noise in the dining hall during Welcome Week?"
  • "How on earth do I get extra time in exams or sort out disability equipment?"
  • "What does an 'Inclusive Support Plan (ISP)' or 'DSA' even mean?"

The STAR (Student Transition and Residential) Programme is a fantastic, free 3-day event held every August to help students settle in before the big crowds arrive. But when offer-holders are nervous at home at 11 PM, they don't want to dig through university PDF documents. They want quick, honest, plain-English answers from students who have been there.

Our Four Core Commitments

🔒 100% Anonymous

We wipe names, phone numbers, and emails with regex filters before processing. No chat history is saved on our server.

🛡️ Immediate Crisis Support

If someone types that they are in distress, the app bypasses general answering and immediately displays emergency helpline numbers.

📚 Grounded in Real Guides

Answers are generated exclusively from approved UCLan web pages. If something isn't in our verified notes, we tell you honestly.

♿ Accessible for Everyone

Tested against WCAG 2.2 AA: high contrast modes (14:1+), dyslexia-friendly fonts, keyboard navigation, and text-to-speech read-aloud.
