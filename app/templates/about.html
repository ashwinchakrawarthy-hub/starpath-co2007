Project Purpose & Academic Background

A student-led agile coursework project developed by second-year undergraduates to assist incoming disabled, neurodivergent, and transition-anxious offer-holders preparing for the UCLan STAR Residential.

Context & Problem Statement

Transitioning from further education to higher education is a significant milestone that frequently causes acute anxiety for offer-holders with declared disabilities, specific learning difficulties, mental health challenges, or those who are first-in-family students.

During our preliminary user research, we identified that essential onboarding information (such as accommodation specifications, catering timings, quiet sensory zones, and disability support registration) is fragmented across multiple university web pages and long PDF handbooks.

The STAR (Student Transition and Residential) Programme is an established 3-day residential event hosted annually by the University of Central Lancashire in August. STARPath was conceived as a grounded, privacy-preserving assistant that provides immediate, citation-backed answers to practical questions without overwhelming offer-holders with bureaucratic terminology.

Architectural & Ethical Principles

1. Anonymity & Privacy (UK GDPR)

Personal identifiers including names, student IDs, UK phone numbers, and email addresses are sanitized using deterministic regex filters before query processing. Server logs record zero personal data.

2. Crisis Intervention Guardrail

Queries containing crisis or self-harm keywords bypass the retrieval engine immediately, returning dedicated signposting cards with clickable telephone links to professional human support.

3. Deterministic Source Grounding

Answers are synthesized exclusively from an approved, dated knowledge base of UCLan web documentation. If information is not present, the assistant returns an honest refusal rather than fabricating answers.

4. Universal Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)

Designed with high-contrast themes (up to 14:1 AAA), scalable font settings, OpenDyslexic typography, keyboard navigation focus indicators, and Web Speech API audio synthesis.
